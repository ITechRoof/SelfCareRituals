# Skin Ritual 💧

Your personal skincare routine, guided step by step on your iPhone.

Skin Ritual knows your routine: which products to use, in what order, how much of each, and how long to let each one dry. Tap **Start routine** and it walks you through. It picks the right routine for the time of day, adjusts portions for the weather, and keeps track of your progress so you can see your consistency build up.

## What it does

- **Picks the right routine:** morning or evening, gym day or not, and which evening routine is due tonight.
- **Guides you one step at a time,** showing the product, how much to use, where it goes and how to apply it.
- **Times the drying for you:** after each step that needs it, a countdown runs and moves you on by itself when it's done.
- **Adjusts for the weather:** summer or winter care, based on today's forecast where you are.
- **Tracks your progress:** morning and evening ticks, streaks, a heatmap of your history and weekly skin notes.
- **Keeps progress photos** with a guide overlay so every photo is taken at the same angle, plus a before-and-after slider.
- **Answers questions** about your routine through an AI chat that knows your plan.
- **Reminds you** with notifications at routine time.

## Getting started

1. **Get your login.** The person who set the app up for you will send you a **link**, a **name** and a **passphrase**.
2. **Install it on your iPhone:**
   1. Open the link in **Safari**.
   2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**.
   3. Make sure **Open as Web App** is switched **on**, then tap **Add**.
   4. From now on, always open Skin Ritual **from its Home Screen icon**.
3. **Sign in** with your name and passphrase. Leave **Stay signed in on this phone** ticked so you only do this once. iPhone can save the passphrase in Passwords for you.
4. **Answer two quick questions:** which days you go to the gym, and your location for the weather.

## Doing your routine

Tap **Start routine** on the Today screen.

- **In the morning,** it asks **Gym or post-gym?**
  - **Pre-gym:** a quick cleanse and sunscreen before you head out.
  - **Post-gym:** your full morning routine after your bath. On days you don't go to the gym, this is your main morning routine.
- **In the evening,** it picks tonight's routine for you. You can switch if needed.

Before you begin, you'll see the steps, today's season and any reminders. Tap **Begin**, then for each step:

- **Done** moves on. If the product needs time to dry, a timer starts: the droplet slowly empties, it chimes when ready, and it moves to the next step on its own.
- **+1 min** gives a little more drying time. **Skip wait** moves on straight away.
- **Skip** leaves out a step today. **Back** returns to the previous one.
- Some steps offer a gentler alternative, for example if your skin is flaking or stinging.

When you finish, the routine is saved automatically. A routine done after midnight counts as the night before, so late nights don't break your streak.

## Changing your routine

If you switch products or want to adjust something, go to **Settings > My routine > Edit my routine**. Tap any step to:

- **Swap the product.** If you use it in other routines too, you can change them all at once.
- **Change the amount,** separately for summer and winter where your plan has both.
- **Change the "how to apply" note.**
- **Turn the step off** if you've stopped using it.

You can also choose **which evenings are retinal nights**. The others become rest nights.

- **Gentle warnings:** the app warns you about changes worth a second thought, like retinal nights back to back or turning off sunscreen, but it never stops you.
- **Undo:** steps you've changed show **Changed**. **Back to my plan's version** undoes one step, and **Undo all my changes** resets everything.
- **Privacy:** your changes are saved on your phone only and included in your backup.
- **Plan updates:** if your plan is updated later, everything you haven't changed follows the update, and your own changes stay.
- **AI chat:** knows about your changes.

## The screens

### Today

- **Today card:** shows what's next, in the routine's own colours, with today's weather and season.
- **Morning and Evening tiles:** what you've done so far today.
- **Order list:** the next routine's steps, with portions and drying times.

### Progress

- **Streak:** the number of days in a row with both your morning and evening routines done.
- **Last 30 days:** how many mornings and evenings you've completed.
- **This week:** a tick for each morning and evening. **Tap a circle** to tick a routine you did without the app.
- **Heatmap:** your last few months at a glance. The top half of each square is the morning and the bottom half is the evening. **Tap a day** to change its ticks.
- **Skin notes:** a few words each week about how your skin is doing.

### Photos

1. Choose an area: face, left side, right side, under-eyes, arms or feet.
2. Tap **Take photo**. Your last photo of that area shows faintly over the camera, so you can line up the same angle and distance.
3. Tap **Compare** to slide between an earlier and a later photo.

Same spot, same light and same time of day make changes easiest to see. Photos stay on your phone.

### Ask

Ask anything about your routine. For example: *"Why do I wait between steps?"* or *"My skin is stinging today, what should I change?"*. It knows your plan, today's weather and what you've logged recently.

This needs your own Anthropic API key, from **console.anthropic.com**. Add it in **Settings > AI chat**. It stays on your phone.

### Settings

- **Theme:** five colour themes (Twilight, Soft pastel, Warm luxe, Fresh spa, Bold jewel). Light or dark follows your iPhone.
- **Gym days** and the time your evening routines start.
- **Weather and season:** use the forecast, or force summer or winter care.
- **My routine:** swap products, change amounts, turn steps off, choose your retinal nights.
- **Drying times:** change or switch off any wait.
- **Reminders:**
  - **Add to Calendar:** repeating calendar alerts. Works for everyone, no setup needed.
  - **Turn on notifications:** real reminders on your phone. After allowing them, tap **Send my address** and send the message to the person who set up the app, so reminders can reach you.
- **Account:** shows who's signed in, with **Sign out** to switch person.
- **Backup:** export your logs, notes and photos to a file, and import them again.

## Your privacy

- **Your routine** is stored encrypted and can only be opened with your passphrase.
- **Your logs, notes, photos and settings stay on your phone.** If someone else uses Skin Ritual on the same phone, their data is kept separate from yours.
- **Weather lookups** send only your approximate location.
- **AI chat** messages go directly from your phone to Anthropic, using your own key.
- **Notifications** use a phone address that only lets reminders reach you. It isn't a password.

Because everything is kept on your phone, **export a backup now and then** (Settings > Backup).

## Tips and troubleshooting

| If… | Try this |
|---|---|
| It asks for setup or sign-in every time | Open it from the **Home Screen icon**, not Safari. Make sure you're not in Private Browsing |
| A red "isn't saving your data" warning appears | Turn off **Settings > Apps > Safari > Advanced > Block All Cookies**, and use the Home Screen icon |
| A red "Open the app from your Home Screen" line shows in Settings | Delete the icon and add it again with **Open as Web App** switched on |
| Your name and passphrase don't work | Check the spelling. Capitals don't matter in the name, but they do in the passphrase. If it still fails, ask for a new passphrase |
| No notification popup appears | You may have answered before. Check **iPhone Settings > Notifications > Skin Ritual**. Notifications need iOS 16.4 or later |
| You're not seeing the latest version | Swipe the app away and open it again, twice |
| You got a new phone or reinstalled | Sign in again, import your backup, and tap **Send my address** again for notifications |
